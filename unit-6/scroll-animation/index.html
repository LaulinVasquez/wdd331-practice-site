Scroll-Driven Animations

This page demonstrates how CSS can connect animation progress to scrolling instead of time.

What this demo shows

The blue bar at the top uses a root scroll timeline. The cards below use view timelines so they animate as each card enters the viewport.

01

Root Scroll Progress

The progress bar grows horizontally as the page scrolls from top to bottom. The animation is driven by scroll position, not seconds.

02

View Timeline

Each card fades and slides into place when it enters the viewport. The card itself becomes the animation trigger.

03

Animation Range

The entrance is limited to a specific part of the card's journey, so the motion finishes before the card is fully settled on screen.

Why this matters

Scroll-driven animations can replace JavaScript scroll listeners for many common effects. They are declarative, smoother, and easier to guard with prefers-reduced-motion.
